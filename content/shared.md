## Repeated FAQ entries
- **Will you finish on time?** Yes. My work stays inside the timeline we agree in the scope. The timeline changes only in two cases: the scope grows, or the project waits for your input (access, files, answers, feedback). If that happens I tell you right away and we agree new dates in writing.
- **What if you don't deliver?** Every project has a Delivery Guarantee: if I don't deliver the agreed scope on time, you get a full refund. I guarantee delivery, not business results.
- **Can you do it faster?** Sometimes. A rush {project}, about half the standard timeline, adds 30% and is only possible when I have an open slot.
- **I already bought a teardown. Do I get credit?** Yes, 50% of the teardown fee if you start within 30 days. (Not shown on the Teardown page itself.)

## Delivery Guarantee
**Short:** You pay for a finished scope, not for promises. If I don't deliver what we agreed on time, you get a full refund.

**Strip (end of each service page, above the final button):** You pay for a finished scope, not for promises. If I don't deliver the agreed scope on time, you get a full refund. I guarantee delivery, not business results.

**Full version (Terms page):** Before any work starts, I send a one-page scope with the deliverables, the dates and a delivery checklist. If I don't deliver everything on that checklist by the agreed date plus 50% of the project timeline, I refund the full project fee. Example: a 2-week project not delivered within 3 weeks is refunded in full.
- Covers: the deliverables written in the signed scope.
- Pauses the clock: waiting for access, files, answers or feedback.
- Does not cover: business results (sales, conversion, revenue), fees paid to third-party tools, anything added after the scope was signed.
- Refund timing: within 7 days of the request.
- If I delivered and the client disputes it, we go through the checklist together.
- Deadline is counted in working days of my work. The clock starts when I have the kickoff package listed in the scope. After 5 working days of silence the project is marked "waiting" and the guarantee does not apply to delays caused by waiting.
