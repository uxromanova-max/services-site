# Original copy notes (not rendered)

## Top
**Pain line:** Your best product page is doing the hardest job in your store. Let's make it easier to buy from.
**After:** A redesigned product page, built or ready to build.
**From $600 · 1 to 2 weeks**

## Sounds familiar?
- People view the product but don't add to cart.
- The page is long, but the answers to common questions are missing.
- It looks fine on desktop and clumsy on mobile.
- Photos, reviews and delivery info are scattered.

## What you get
- New page structure: gallery, headline, benefits, variants, trust elements, FAQ, reviews, cross-sell.
- Copy blocks for key sections (sharpened from your existing copy, or written, depending on the package).
- Design for desktop and mobile.
- Built into your Shopify theme (higher packages) or delivered as design and build notes (base package).
- A reusable layout your team can copy to other products.

## How it works
1. Days 1 to 2: kickoff call, review of the current page and customer questions.
2. Days 3 to 5: structure and design, you review.
3. Days 6 to 9: build or build notes, one round of changes.
4. Day 10: live, handoff and a short how-to.

## What I need from you
- Store access, product photos and any video.
- Reviews, FAQs and common support questions.
- One person who gives feedback within 2 working days.

## Tools
Shopify theme editor, Figma, your reviews and subscription apps if you use them, analytics (GA4, Microsoft Clarity or Hotjar, if connected to your store), PageSpeed Insights for speed checks, Judge.me or Junip for reviews, Skio for subscriptions.

## Price: $600 to $1,500, depends on scope and who supplies content
- **$600 Design:** one product page, design for desktop and mobile, build notes. You or your developer builds it.
- **$1,000 Design + build (most common):** the same, built live in your theme.
- **$1,500 Full:** design, build, new copy and image layouts, plus the layout applied to 2 more products.
- Goes up with: more products, custom-coded sections, bundles or complex variants, no ready photos.

## Proof
Before and after of a product page, mobile view, section breakdown. Related work: ORA Studio, ModaBlvck. (CHECK: screenshots not provided yet)

## Questions
- **Do you write code?** Yes, when the project needs it. Most of the work happens with theme sections and apps. When the design calls for a custom section or a new page, I build it in Liquid. I tell you at the start what is standard and what needs custom code.
- **Can you do my whole catalog?** Yes, as a Store Refresh.
- Will you finish on time? / What if you don't deliver? / Can you do it faster? (rush sprint) / I already bought a teardown. Do I get credit?: see shared.md
- Care window after handoff: 14 days.
