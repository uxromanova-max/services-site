# Original copy notes (not rendered)

## Top
**Pain line:** Your store was fine a year ago. Now it holds you back.
**After:** A cleaner, faster-to-shop Shopify store that looks like your brand.
**From $1,500 · 2 to 4 weeks**

## Sounds familiar?
- The store grew by patches and nobody wants to touch it.
- Navigation is confusing, collections are messy.
- Mobile is an afterthought.
- It no longer looks like your brand.

## What you get
- New structure: navigation, collections, page hierarchy.
- Redesigned homepage, collection pages and product page template.
- Mobile-first polish across the store.
- Product and collection cleanup (titles, images, tags, ordering).
- Handoff guide so your team can update it.
- Recommendations for apps that support conversion (for example reviews with Judge.me or Junip, subscriptions with Skio, upsells, search, shipping bars), and I can set them up for you.

## How it works
1. Week 1: kickoff, store review, structure and design direction approved.
2. Week 2: design of key pages, one round of changes.
3. Weeks 3 to 4: build in the theme, content cleanup, mobile checks.
4. Launch: go live, final checks, handoff.

## What I need from you
- Store access and a list of what must stay (apps, policies, special pages).
- Brand assets and product photos.
- A single decision-maker and feedback within 2 working days.

## Tools
Shopify and its theme editor, Figma, Photoshop for image prep, your existing apps. I also use analytics reports (GA4, Shopify analytics, Triple Whale, Microsoft Clarity or Hotjar, whatever you have connected) and PageSpeed Insights for speed checks, so the reports I can use depend on what is set up in your store.

## Price: $1,500 to $4,000, depends on pages, products and how much is new
- **$1,500 Refresh:** homepage, collection template, product page template on your current theme.
- **$2,500 Rebuild light (most common):** the above plus navigation, mobile polish and cleanup of up to ~30 products.
- **$4,000 Full rebuild:** new theme setup, up to ~8 key pages, structure from scratch, content cleanup, on-site SEO setup (page titles, meta descriptions, URLs, image alt text, structured data basics) and a recommended list of conversion apps that I can also install and configure.
- Goes up with: number of products and collections, multiple markets or languages, custom-coded pages or sections, and large custom features (scoped separately).

## Questions
- **Will you change my theme?** Only if it makes sense. A refresh is often cheaper on your current theme.
- **Do you do custom code?** Yes, when the project needs it. I often add pages and sections that are not part of the standard theme. Before we start I tell you what is standard theme work and what needs custom code.
