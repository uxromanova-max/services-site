# Original copy notes (not rendered)

## Top
**Pain line:** Your customers are leaving without buying, and no email goes after them.
**After:** Automated emails that welcome, recover and re-engage customers, plus a reusable email template system for every future flow.
**From $500 · 1 to 2 weeks**

## Sounds familiar?
- You send a campaign now and then, but nothing runs on its own.
- Abandoned carts get no reminder, or only a plain one.
- New subscribers get one generic welcome and then silence.
- Your emails look different from your store.

## What you get
- Flow plan: which emails, when, and why.
- Written and designed emails in your brand style. They are built as reusable templates: a small email design system (header, product blocks, offers, footer) that you can use for every future flow and campaign, so new emails take minutes instead of days.
- Custom design for every email in the flows, not a generic template with your logo.
- Flows built, tested and switched on in your email platform.
- A simple map of the flows so your team understands them.

## How it works
1. Days 1 to 2: audit of your list and what you send now.
2. Days 3 to 4: flow plan and email copy for your approval.
3. Days 5 to 8: design and build in the platform.
4. Days 9 to 10: test, go live, handoff.

## What I need from you
- Access to your email platform and store.
- Brand assets (logo, colors, fonts, photos).
- Your discount rules and shipping/returns info.

## Tools
Your email platform (Klaviyo, Omnisend, MailerLite or Shopify), Shopify, Figma or the platform's editor for design.

## Price: $500 to $1,200, depends on the number of flows and design level
- **$500 Core 3:** welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, on a clean template in your brand.
- **$900 Core 5 (most common):** adds browse abandonment and winback, with custom-designed emails.
- **$1,200 Full:** the above plus a campaign template kit and a flow map.
- Extra flow: $150 each. Extra custom-designed email: $150 to $250.
- Goes up with: more segments, multiple languages, SMS flows, no brand assets.

## Questions
- **Will it increase revenue?** Flows work on the people who are already on your list, so results depend on how large and how engaged that list is. I can't promise a specific number up front. What I do is improve how those subscribers convert, and I set up tracking so you can see what each flow earns.
- **Can you run campaigns too?** Not in this package. We can talk about monthly support afterward.
- **Can my team reuse the email designs?** Yes. That is the point of building them as templates. After the project you can duplicate them for new flows and campaigns without a designer.
