## Delivery Guarantee

Before any work starts, I send a one-page scope with the deliverables, the dates and a delivery checklist. If I don't deliver everything on that checklist by the agreed date plus 50% of the project timeline, I refund the full project fee.
**Example:** a 2-week project that is not delivered within 3 weeks is refunded in full.

**What it covers:** the deliverables written in the signed scope.

**What pauses the clock:** waiting for your access, files, answers or feedback (rule: if there is no reply for 5 working days, the clock stops until you reply).

**What it does not cover:** business results (sales, conversion, revenue), fees you already paid to third-party tools, and anything added after the scope was signed.

**Refund timing:** within 7 days of the request.
